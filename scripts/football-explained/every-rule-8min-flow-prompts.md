# Every Football Rule Explained — Google Flow (Veo) Prompts

Motion prompts rewritten for **Google Flow / Veo**. Each shot lists its **duration**
and a self-contained cinematic prompt. Built to match the VO lines in
`every-rule-8min.md` / `every-rule-8min-shotlist.md`.

---

## How to use this in Google Flow (free)

1. **Free credits:** Flow gives a limited monthly free allotment. Generate in batches,
   pick the best take, and re-roll only failures to stretch credits. Render at the
   lower resolution while testing; upscale only keepers.
2. **Clip length:** Veo clips are ~8 seconds. For shots shorter than that, generate the
   8s clip and **trim in your editor** to the listed duration. For shots longer than 8s
   (S50, S66), generate two clips and use Flow's **"Extend"** on the first.
3. **Character consistency (the narrator):** Use Flow's **"Ingredients to Video."** Make
   one reference image of the presenter (prompt in the NARRATOR block below), save it as
   an Ingredient, and attach it to every presenter shot so he looks identical throughout.
4. **No talking:** Veo generates audio and will invent speech/captions. Every prompt ends
   with **"no spoken dialogue, no on-screen text, ambient sound only"** — keep that. Then
   **mute Veo's audio** in your edit and lay your own VO on top.
5. **Title cards:** Don't rely on Veo to spell words correctly. Generate the card shots as
   clean animated backgrounds, then **add the actual text in your editor** (CapCut,
   Premiere, DaVinci). Card prompts below describe the background only.
6. **Aspect ratio:** set **16:9** for all shots (long-form YouTube).

**STYLE TAG (keep at the end of every prompt):**
> *Style: bright, high-saturation 3D cartoon explainer animation; clean minimalist
> design; soft global illumination; bold flat colors; smooth, energetic motion;
> 16:9. No spoken dialogue, no on-screen text, ambient sound only.*

**NARRATOR INGREDIENT (make a reference image with this, reuse everywhere he appears):**

## Workflow tips for building the channel on free credits
- **Batch the title cards last** — they're cheap backgrounds; spend credits on the hero shots first.
- **Reuse Ingredients** for the narrator AND for the pitch/stadium look so every video in the
  series shares one visual identity (huge for channel branding).
- **Trim, don't regenerate.** Most shots only need 4–6s of an 8s clip — generate once, cut to length.
- **Lay your VO and the on-screen text in the editor**, not in Veo — cheaper, accurate, and editable.
- **Save winning prompts** per shot so future episodes (positions, trophies, tactics) stay consistent.

## Accuracy checklist (verify vs current IFAB Laws before recording VO)
- Substitution count (up to 5 in most competitions)
- Added-time / extra-time mechanics
- Handball wording (deliberate vs. unnatural position)
- Offside phrasing (second-to-last defender; judged at the moment of the pass)
